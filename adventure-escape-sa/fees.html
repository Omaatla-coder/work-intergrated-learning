What’s included

• All technical equipment (harnesses, helmets, kayaks, ropes, etc.)

• Professional certified guide

• Comprehensive safety briefing

• Public liability insurance for the activity

• Light refreshments or picnic (depending on package)

Not included: Transport to meeting points (unless stated in package), personal travel insurance, tips, and accommodation (except Weekend Escape package).

Discounts & policies

Groups of 8+ — 10% off individual activity rates.

Children (8–15) — 15% off when accompanied by a paying adult.

Cancellation: Full refund up to 48 hours before the activity. 50% refund within 48 hours. No refund for no-shows.

Weather: Activities may be rescheduled or refunded if conditions make them unsafe. We will contact you as early as possible.
