About Adventure Escape SA

We’re a team of outdoor enthusiasts based in the Western Cape, passionate about sharing the wild beauty of South Africa with every guest.

Our story

Founded in 2024, Adventure Escape SA was born from a simple idea: make world-class outdoor experiences accessible, safe, and unforgettable. From the forest canopies of the Garden Route to the calm waters of the Breede River and the granite faces of the Cape Fold mountains, we design guided adventures that connect people with nature and with each other.

Every trip is led by certified local guides who know the terrain, the weather, and the stories behind each place. We keep groups small so you get personal attention and space to breathe.

What drives us

🌲

Nature first

We leave no trace, support local conservation, and choose routes that respect the land and wildlife.

🛡️

Safety always

Certified guides, quality gear, and clear briefings mean you can focus on the thrill, not the worry.

🤝

Real connection

Small groups and shared challenges create friendships and memories that last long after the trip ends.

Where we operate

All of our experiences are based in the Western Cape — from Stellenbosch and Franschhoek to the Overberg, Garden Route, and the Cape Peninsula. Whether you’re a first-timer or a seasoned adventurer, we’ll match you with the right activity and level.
