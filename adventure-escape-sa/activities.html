Our activities

From flying through the trees to paddling calm rivers and scaling rock faces — pick the adventure that matches your energy.

Ziplining

Duration: 2–3 hours · Difficulty: Easy–Moderate · Age: 8+

Soar above the forest canopy on a series of long, scenic ziplines. Our courses are designed for first-timers and families, with dual lines so you can race a friend and professional harness systems for complete peace of mind.

Kayaking

Duration: 2–4 hours · Difficulty: Easy · Age: 10+

Paddle the calm waters of the Breede River or sheltered coastal lagoons. We provide stable sit-on-top kayaks, paddles, and life jackets. Perfect for couples, friends, and anyone who wants to find their flow on the water.

R750 Book kayaking

Rock climbing

Duration: 3–4 hours · Difficulty: Moderate · Age: 12+

Reach a new high on carefully selected outdoor routes in the Cape Fold mountains. Top-rope and beginner-friendly multi-pitch options available. All gear and instruction included — no prior experience required.
